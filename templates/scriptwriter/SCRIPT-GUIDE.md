# Script guide: {{name}}

Channel rules (audience, level, cast, places, never-list) are in `CHANNEL.md`. This file is the craft: format, structure, pacing and the self-check.

## Format

```
[STORY]
MIA: Good morning! Something smells amazing in here.
WALTER: That's the cinnamon rolls. They just came out of the oven.
[LESSON]
NARRATOR: "Something smells amazing" is a friendly way to start a conversation.
```

- Segment markers on their own line, in square brackets, capitals: `[COLD OPEN]`, `[STORY]`, `[LESSON]`, `[GRAMMAR CORNER]`, `[STORY SLOW]`, `[REPEAT]`, `[QUIZ]`, `[OUTRO]`.
- Every other line: `NAME: text`, NAME in capitals from the cast in `CHANNEL.md` (first names: `MIA`, `WALTER`, `GRACE`, `LENA`, `TOM`, `ROSA`, `OMAR`, `JUNE`, `NARRATOR`).
- One speaker turn per line (1-3 short sentences). The studio numbers these lines (L1, L2 ...) for the scene plan.
- Character lines in `[STORY SLOW]` and `[REPEAT]` are slowed down automatically. After every character line in `[REPEAT]` there is an automatic silent pause for the viewer to speak.
- Write prices and times as people say them when the price is spoken ("That'll be six dollars", "three fifty").

## 16:9 episode: 6-9 minutes

| Segment | Lines | Job |
|---|---|---|
| `[COLD OPEN]` | 2-3 | A funny or tense moment from the story, then the narrator: "Episode N. Title. At Place." |
| `[STORY]` | 12-20 | The whole scene at natural speed. Every target phrase appears at least once. A small problem and a warm ending. |
| `[LESSON]` | 15-30 | For each target phrase: "Phrase N. ..." → the character's line again → the meaning in simple English → one more example. Include one "Don't say ... Say ..." if it fits. |
| `[GRAMMAR CORNER]` | 2-4 | Optional. One pattern from the story, 2-3 examples, no grammar tables. |
| `[STORY SLOW]` | same as STORY | The story again, word for word. |
| `[REPEAT]` | 6-10 | "Your turn. You are Mia. Listen and repeat." → phrase → praise → phrase → praise ... → one question from another character for the viewer to answer → praise. |
| `[QUIZ]` | 6 | 3 questions, each followed by its answer line. |
| `[OUTRO]` | 2-3 | Close a joke from the cold open, tease the next place, then the CTA line from `CHANNEL.md`. |

## Shorts (9:16): 35-55 seconds, 8-14 lines

Pick one pattern from `CHANNEL.md` (Don't say / Say, mini-dialogue, "What does ... mean?", quiz). Structure: 2-6 dialogue lines → the phrase explained in 1-2 narrator lines → a quick `[REPEAT]` of the phrase with praise → the CTA line.

## What the studio can show (use these words in `scene_plan.type`)

| Type | Shows | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| `dialogue` | 1-3 characters in a place; the speaker talks and gets the speech bubble | `[STORY]`, `[STORY SLOW]`, cold open (3-6 lines per scene) |
| `location` | the street, the camera flies to a house or shop; episode title and level badge | the episode title, a change of place |
| `repeat` | the phrase card, mic countdown during the pause, praise burst | `[REPEAT]` (the whole segment in 1-2 scenes) |
| `bigtext` | big words, one highlighted | a phrase card in `[LESSON]` |
| `versus` | A vs B cards with a winner | "Don't say / Say", "for here / to go" |
| `list` | up to 5 rows | grammar corner, quiz choices, recap of phrases |
| `talk` | {{character}} with a speech bubble | one-line explanations |
| `title` | a big title card | chapter titles |
| `icons`, `steps`, `outro` | see the studio reference | the last scene is always `outro` |

A `dialogue` scene may run up to ~20 s and a `repeat` scene up to ~30 s; other scenes 3-9 s.

## Self-check before showing the script

- [ ] Every line has a speaker tag from the cast or is a segment marker
- [ ] Every target phrase is said in `[STORY]` and explained in `[LESSON]`
- [ ] Words outside the target phrases fit the level; story lines ≤ ~12 words
- [ ] `[STORY SLOW]` repeats `[STORY]` word for word
- [ ] Every pause in `[REPEAT]` is followed by a different short praise
- [ ] Nothing contradicts the cast, places or prices in `CHANNEL.md`
- [ ] No translations, no brands, nothing from the "Never" list
- [ ] Last line is the channel CTA
