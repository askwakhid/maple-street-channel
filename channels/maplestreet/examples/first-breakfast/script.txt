NARRATOR: It's a friendly way to start a conversation in a café or a kitchen.

NARRATOR: Phrase two. What can I get you?
WALTER: So, what can I get you?
NARRATOR: The person at the counter asks this. It means, what do you want to order?
NARRATOR: Phrase three. Can I get a coffee, please?

NARRATOR: Phrase four. Anything else?

NARRATOR: The person wants to know if you want one more thing.
